About EnvisionVR Technology

Bringing Tomorrow's Technology To Businesses Today

EnvisionVR Technology is a technology solutions company providing innovative AV, IT, and AR/VR/MR services tailored for modern businesses and institutions. We help organizations move beyond flat screens and static presentations into fully immersive, interactive digital experiences.

From boardrooms that need seamless audio-visual integration to training programs that demand realistic virtual simulations, our team designs, installs, and supports technology built around how your business actually works.

Based in Balasore, Odisha, we work with clients across sectors — corporate offices, educational institutions, retail spaces, and event organizers — bringing immersive technology within reach.
